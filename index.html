THE NOIR & SAGE PHILOSOPHY

"Great food doesn’t
need to shout."

At Noir & Sage, every plate begins with restraint — honest ingredients, deliberate technique and the warmth of an open flame. We create food that feels familiar at first bite, then leaves something unexpected behind.

Founded on the belief that dining is an art of slow living, our kitchen honors the elemental connection between raw fire and botanical harmony. We forage locally, curate small-batch produce from regenerative farms, and pair each creation with cellar-aged wines and smoked elixirs.

THE HAND BEHIND THE PLATE

ARJUN MEHRA

"Chef Arjun approaches contemporary dining with one principle: complexity should never come at the expense of comfort."

Trained across celebrated kitchens in San Sebastián, Kyoto, and Copenhagen, Arjun returned to India driven by a single desire: to strip away culinary pretense and celebrate the raw, primal essence of firewood and time.

At Noir & Sage, there are no artificial shortcuts. Sauces simmer over residual coals for sixteen hours; sea bass is seared over fragrant oak wood; and wild sage is harvested daily. Every dish honors the delicate balance between restraint and sensory impact.

"Cook with instinct. Finish with intention." Arjun Mehra
